01 — About

Get to know me

I'm Lawal Olayinka Awal — known online as Ayinkx. I'm a Python & backend developer, a Nigerian musician & producer and a content creator. Different worlds, same person: I love creating things people genuinely connect with.

On the tech side I focus on Python, Flask and REST APIs, with a growing toolkit around Docker, SQL and automation — plus open source contributions to Stellar/Soroban and Python projects. My journey is fully self-taught and learn-by-building.

As Ayinkx / Ayinkxreacts, I make music (love songs, chill vibes, real stories) and create reaction videos & live streams for a growing community. Whether it's code, a song or a video, I'm here to build and to connect.
